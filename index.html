KnotLogic Drive — rclone

A personal Google Drive connection maintained by the owner of the KnotLogic GitHub account. It uses the open-source rclone application to make the owner's Google Drive files available as local folders on the owner's Ubuntu computer.

This OAuth client is intended for the owner's own Google accounts. It is not a public file-hosting service, and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google or the rclone project.

The application can list, read, download, upload, modify, and delete Google Drive files when instructed by its owner. Files are cached locally as needed; this does not automatically create a complete offline copy of Google Drive.
